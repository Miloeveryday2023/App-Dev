***Crash Landing on You*** is a South Korean romantic drama about a South Korean heiress, Yoon Se-ri, who accidentally crash-lands in North Korea after a paragliding accident. There, she meets Ri Jeong-hyeok, a North Koream army officer who decides to protect her. Despite their differences and the political divide, they slowly fall in love while trying to find a way to bring her back home safely.
